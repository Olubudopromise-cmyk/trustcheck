## Version

0.1

---

# Vision

TrustCheck is an AI-powered verification platform that helps people determine whether digital entities are legitimate, trustworthy, and safe to interact with.

Instead of only asking:

"Does it exist?"

TrustCheck answers:

- Does it exist?
- Can it be trusted?
- What are the risks?
- Why?
- What evidence supports this?

---

# Mission

Build the world's most trusted verification platform.

---

# Problem Statement

Every day people receive:

- Scam websites
- Fake phone numbers
- Fraudulent emails
- Fake businesses
- Phishing links
- Suspicious crypto wallets

Most people don't know whether they are safe.

TrustCheck provides one place to verify them.
